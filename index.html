Machine Effectiveness Analysis with OEE

PT Mayora Indah Tbk, Jatake 2

Measured Overall Equipment Effectiveness of Packing Machine A7 on the Roma Lavita biscuit line over seven production weeks (Nov–Dec 2024), obtaining an average OEE of 21% against the 85% world-class standard, with availability 71%, performance efficiency 30%, and quality rate 99.98%. Six Big Losses analysis identified breakdown losses (28.57%) as the dominant contributor, confirmed through Pareto and fishbone diagrams, leading to proposals for preventive and predictive maintenance plus One Point Lesson (OPL) operator training.
